Retainer

Mail, calendar and notes, organised by client.

Retainer is a private tool built for one independent consultant who works for several clients at once.

What it does

  • Brings mail from several Google accounts into one inbox, with each message tagged by the client it belongs to.
  • Sorts mail by whether it needs a reply, how urgent it is, and whether it is a receipt.
  • Keeps a knowledge base for each client, built from mail, documents and meeting notes.

Who can use it

Retainer is not a public product. It is used only by its owner, with Google accounts the owner controls or has been given by their clients. There is no sign-up, and no one else's data is collected.

Google data

Retainer asks Google for access to read Gmail and, when its owner asks, to archive mail or move it to the Bin. It does not send mail. How that data is handled is set out in the privacy policy.
