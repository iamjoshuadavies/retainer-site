Retainer

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 (Google Contacts and Drive access added).

Who this covers

Retainer is a private tool used by a single person, its owner, Josh Davies. It is not offered to the public. This policy describes how Retainer handles data from the Google accounts its owner connects to it.

What Retainer accesses

  • Gmail, read and organise (gmail.modify): messages, their headers and labels, and the account's email address.
  • Retainer changes mail only when its owner asks it to: archiving, marking as read or unread, and moving to the Bin, where Gmail keeps mail for 30 days.
  • Retainer sends mail only when its owner writes a reply and presses Send, and never permanently deletes mail.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly and calendar.events): the account's calendars and their events (titles, times, places, attendees and notes), so they can be shown together and organised by client. Retainer writes to a calendar only to add plain "busy" blocks that mirror the owner's time on their other calendars, with no details, and keeps them up to date. It does not change or delete any other event, and does not change calendar settings or sharing.
  • Google Contacts and directory, read only (contacts.readonly, contacts.other.readonly and directory.readonly): the names, photos, job titles and phone numbers of the owner's contacts, of people the owner has emailed, and of colleagues in the account's own organisation, so the people the owner deals with at each client can be shown with their details. Retainer never changes contacts.
  • Google Drive, read only (drive.readonly): files the owner can open in Drive, so documents that people send or link to can be opened and filed with the right client. Retainer never changes, shares or deletes files.

How the data is used

Mail, calendar events, contacts and Drive files are used only to show them to the owner in Retainer and to organise them, in Retainer and, when the owner asks, in Gmail: working out which client a message belongs to, whether it needs a reply, how urgent it is, and whether it is a receipt. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

  • Mail, calendar events, contact details, filed documents and sign-in tokens are stored on a server the owner controls.
  • They are encrypted at rest, with a separate key for each connected account.
  • Only the owner can reach that server.

Who else processes it

To sort mail, the text of each message is sent to OpenAI's API, which returns judgements such as "needs a reply". OpenAI processes it under its API terms, which do not allow the data to be used to train its models. No other third party receives Google data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Retainer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while an account stays connected. Disconnecting an account, or revoking Retainer's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, stops all further access, and the owner deletes that account's stored mail and tokens. To ask about data from your account, contact the address below.
